About BIOCARE

A multidisciplinary team of innovators transforming healthcare through cutting-edge technology

Pioneering Healthcare IT Solutions

BIOCARE Diagnostics Technologies represents the convergence of medical expertise and technological innovation. Founded by a distinguished team of computer engineers and biomedical specialists, we specialize in developing sophisticated software solutions for medical laboratories and diagnostic centers.

Our operations are based at the prestigious ASI Innovative IT Start-Ups Incubator in Ciudad Real, providing us with access to cutting-edge infrastructure including dedicated CPD facilities and high-speed 600MB duplex connectivity. This strategic environment enables us to develop and deploy enterprise-grade solutions efficiently.

Our Competitive Advantages

  • Multidisciplinary team combining engineering excellence with biomedical expertise
  • Proven academic and professional experience in clinical information systems
  • Strategic investment from private equity funds specialized in healthcare innovation
  • State-of-the-art 100m² facility with dedicated CPD infrastructure access
  • Enterprise-grade connectivity (600MB duplex) ensuring optimal performance
  • Unwavering commitment to data security, integrity, and regulatory compliance
